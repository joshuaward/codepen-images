The gentle case for one extra day

Why leave on Thursday, August 27?

This isn't about squeezing in more miles for me. It's about how we feel when we land — rested, unhurried, and waking up to a weekend on Lake Como instead of a travel day lost to jet lag and workweek rhythm.

Arrive on Friday

Leave Thursday, land Friday in Milan. Our first real days abroad are the weekend — when the lake is alive, the light is golden, and we're not rushing through menus between meetings we don't have.

Weekend at Lake Como

Menaggio, boat taxis, Bellagio villas, and that bridge in Nesso — all timed for when Como feels like a vacation, not a checkbox on a Tuesday.

Weekday Dolomites

Seceda and Carezza when the trails are quieter. Better photos, calmer ridgelines, and more room at Rifugio Friedrich August for those famous donuts.

Return on Saturday

Fly home Saturday, September 12 — so we keep all of Sunday to unpack, rest, and ease back before Monday. No landing Sunday night and starting the week exhausted.

Yes, Thursday–Saturday fits my Monday–Friday schedule — but I chose these dates because they unlock the most magic for us. If a different day works better for you, we change it together.

Stop 1 · Weekend on the water

Lake Como

You'll wake up in Menaggio with the Alps reflected on the lake. We'll hop water taxis from village to village, tour the villas of Bellagio, and — if you're brave — find the famous diving spot at the Ponte di Nesso.

  • Stay in Menaggio — quieter charm, easy ferry access
  • Boat taxis around the lake from one picturesque shore to the next
  • Jump off the bridge in Nesso (optional — I'll go first)
  • Villa del Balbianello & Villa Melzi gardens in Bellagio
  • Varenna's lakeside promenade and gelato at sunset
Proposed: 2 days · Arrive Friday, explore Saturday–Sunday

Stop 2 · The scenic transition

Drive to Bolzano

One of the most beautiful drives in Europe — from alpine lakes through the Brenner corridor into South Tyrol, where Italian espresso meets Austrian alpine culture and the Dolomites rise like cathedrals of stone.

  • Window-down moments through mountain passes
  • Bolzano's medieval arcades and Ötzi Museum
  • First taste of canederli and speck in the Dolomites foothills
Proposed: Scenic full-day drive · Monday

Stop 3 · Weekday magic

The Italian Dolomites

This is why Thursday matters — we're hiking Seceda on a weekday when the ridgeline breathes. We'll see Lago di Carezza's emerald water, stay a night at Rifugio Friedrich August, and earn those legendary donuts at altitude.

  • Seceda ridgeline hike — the famous "ocean of peaks" panorama
  • Lago di Carezza (Karersee) — rainbow lake, 20-minute loop
  • Optional: Lago di Braies — the postcard lake (if we want the crowds)
  • Overnight at Rifugio Friedrich August + their famous donuts
  • Less crowded trails — better photos, more solitude
Proposed: 2 days · Tuesday–Wednesday in the mountains

Stop 4 · The floating city

Venice

We drop the rental and surrender to canals. A gondola at golden hour, cicchetti in a bacaro, house wine that costs less than bottled water at home — and nowhere to be except lost together in the world's most romantic maze.

  • Drop rental — foot and vaporetto only
  • Romantic gondola ride through quiet canals
  • St. Mark's Basilica & Doge's Palace (book ahead)
  • Rialto Market morning, cicchetti crawl by evening
  • Get lost in Castello — the neighborhood tourists skip
Proposed: 2–3 days · Thursday–Friday

Stop 5 · Adriatic weekend

Dubrovnik, Croatia

One-way flight to King's Landing. We stay in the Old Town, walk the city walls at sunset, claim a spot on Plaža Banje, and yes — I'll sit on the Iron Throne like the Game of Thrones geek I proudly am. You get the sun; I get five minutes of nerd joy.

  • Old Town Dubrovnik — marble streets, sea on every side
  • Iron Throne & Lovrijenac Fortress (GoT filming locations)
  • City walls walk — best views in the Adriatic
  • Plaža Banje beach — swim, sun, relax
  • Fresh seafood and Croatian wine on the terrace
Proposed: Weekend stay · 2–3 days

Stop 6 · Day trip

Herceg Novi, Montenegro

A boat day across the Bay of Kotor — another country, another coastline, without changing our base. Herceg Novi's fortresses, seafood by the water, and views that make you forget you're on a schedule at all.

  • Boat or ferry from Dubrovnik — bay scenery the whole way
  • Herceg Novi old town & seaside promenade
  • Kotor Bay views — fjord-like beauty
  • Lunch with Montenegrin wine — back by evening
Proposed: Day trip · one golden day

Stop 7 · Italy's hidden coast

Bari & Polignano a Mare

The gem of Puglia before the world fully discovers it. Polignano's white cliffs plunge into turquoise water; Bari vecchia serves orecchiette made by grandmothers in doorways. We slow down here — on purpose.

  • Polignano a Mare — white Adriatic medieval coastal city
  • Lama Monachile beach & cliffside views
  • Bari Old Town — pasta made in plain sight
  • One-way flight or ferry from Dubrovnik
Proposed: Slow stay · 2–3 days in Polignano

Stop 8 · Grand finale

Naples & the Amalfi Coast

High-speed train south to where Italy gets loud, delicious, and vertical. We hike the crater of Mt. Vesuvius and stand where Pompeii fell in 79 AD. Positano's pastel cliffs, a boat day to Capri, and a drink at the top of the chair lift — the coast everyone dreams about.

  • Mt. Vesuvius summit hike — crater views over the bay
  • Pompeii or Herculaneum — history under your feet
  • Positano — colorful hillside architecture
  • Boat ferry day trip to Capri
  • Chair lift to the top — drink with the whole coast at your feet
  • Fly home from Naples · Saturday Sep 12
Proposed: 3–4 days · Amalfi finale before we fly home
